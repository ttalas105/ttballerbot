# ttballerbot

A personal Discord bot for friends in Home of the Homies. It plays music, manages a shared queue, and provides Rajan's consented AI voice through typed requests or the opt-in “Hey Rajan” wake phrase.

Rajan is a sarcastic friend: short conversational replies, playful roasts, natural profanity, no forced greetings or catchphrases. Music and effects remain available.

Spectator mode lets a participant on the bot's Windows computer select a visible game, video, or Discord stream pop-out. It sends recent screenshots to Gemini and speaks short reactions in the voice channel. It does not receive Discord Go Live video directly. No stream audio or microphone is captured by the companion. Frames and live voice requests are not stored on disk. Provider disclosures must remain visible.

Success means easy pairing, an honest preview, fast relevant reactions, immediate stop controls, and voice commands taking priority. Free configured providers remain selected; no paid fallback. Stream reactions generate silently and discard stale results.
